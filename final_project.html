Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The layout shifts gracefully to narrow screens with responsive grid and flex styling.

Yes. Products update instantly when the user changes category or sort options.

Can I keep my todo list?

Yes. The app stores tasks in localStorage so they remain even after closing or refreshing the browser.
